A few quick answers

Questions?

Does Dropshake move my files into the shelf?

No. Files stay in their original folders while they’re on the shelf. Drag them out to the destination you want.

Which Macs can run Dropshake?

Dropshake supports macOS 13 and later on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

How do I install it?

Download and open the DMG, then drag Dropshake into Applications. macOS may ask you to approve the first launch because the app is not notarized. See the setup steps in the README.

How do updates work?

Packaged releases check for new versions and offer to install them from inside the app. You can also check manually from the menu bar.

Is Dropshake open source?

Yes. Dropshake is available under GPL-3.0-or-later. Browse the source on GitHub.
